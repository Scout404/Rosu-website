A Local Favorite Since Always

Nestled in the vibrant heart of Apoera, our cafe is more than just a place to eat—it's a gathering spot for neighbors and travelers alike. We pride ourselves on using locally sourced ingredients to bring you the authentic flavors of our village. Whether you're here for your morning coffee or a hearty afternoon sandwich, you'll always find a warm smile and a cozy seat waiting for you.
