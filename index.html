About Us

Welcome to Rosu Cafe, a charming establishment nestled in the beautiful village of Apoera. We take pride in serving authentic homecooked meals made with love and the freshest local ingredients.

Our commitment is to bring you the flavors of our region, prepared with traditional recipes passed down through generations. Every dish tells a story of our community and heritage.
