A gathering place for neighbours and travellers alike.

A Local Favorite Since Always

Nestled in the vibrant heart of Apoera, our cafe is more than just a place to eat—it's a gathering spot for neighbours and travellers alike. We pride ourselves on using locally sourced ingredients to bring you the authentic flavours of our village. Whether you're here for your morning coffee or a hearty afternoon sandwich, you'll always find a warm smile and a cosy seat waiting for you.
